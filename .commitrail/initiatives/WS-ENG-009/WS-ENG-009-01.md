# WS-ENG-009-01 — Atomic Commitrail cutover

- Initiative: `WS-ENG-009`
- Durable disposition: Complete
- Intended merge outcome: Commitrail becomes Workstream's only active
  engineering method and `.agent-loop` is removed.

## Intent

Reduce contribution and review complexity without weakening bounded scope,
evidence adequacy, exact-target review, CI protection, or human merge authority.

## Bounded change

Process records, repository guidance, skills, reviewer configuration, CI
validation, tests, and reference relocation only. No Workstream product,
runtime, database, migration, or API behavior changes.

## Acceptance criteria

- `.commitrail` is the single active record location.
- Current initiative navigation and normative facts have explicit custody.
- Every existing Agent Gates protection has an executable successor.
- Positive and negative validator tests cover the supported record model.
- `.agent-loop` and its retired authority/queue/projection machinery cannot
  return without failing CI.
- Other worktrees can rebase and translate only their current bounded change.

## Risk and review routing

- Risk class: L1 engineering process and CI integrity.
- Required reviewers: architecture, CI integrity, documentation, QA,
  reuse/deduplication, security, and senior engineering.
- Human focus: loss of normative truth, hidden dual systems, weakened gates,
  and contributor usability.

## Evidence

- Commitrail contract, permanent legacy-path rejection, reviewer contracts,
  stale-review contracts, stale authorization and artifact contracts, and
  Markdown-link validation pass.
- The repository-wide stale wording scan passes.
- The reviewer and lightweight-gate suites pass: 122 tests.
- The relocation inventory covers all 1,366 paths tracked under `.agent-loop`
  at the exact cutover base.
- Ten continuing initiatives plus the roadmap cutover evidence retain all 752
  pre-cutover initiative files verbatim, including planning, chunk contracts,
  evidence, and reviews. A
  source/destination/blob manifest and Agent Gates permanently enforce their
  byte-identical Git-object proof.
- The relocation proof is bound to the actual pull-request base. A rebase that
  adds or changes legacy records fails until the inventory, exact archive, and
  pinned manifest are reconciled to that base.
- Backend module and authorization architecture tests pass: 92 tests. The
  targeted trusted-ledger relocation regression also passes in the test-
  structure boundary suite.
- AUTH import, test-structure, and behavior-ownership boundary validators pass.
- Ruff passes for every changed Python enforcement path.

Exact reviewed commits are recorded in transient review receipts, not in this
self-changing record. GitHub owns actual checks, approval, and merge state.
