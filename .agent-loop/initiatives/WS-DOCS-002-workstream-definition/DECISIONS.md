# Decisions

## Workstream Is A Standalone Governed Lifecycle

Workstream is not defined by Flow, one marketplace, one industry, one execution
environment, or one economic system. Flow Identity is the current v0.1 external
authentication provider.

## Contribution Facts Are The Durable Output

The canonical durable output is an immutable `ContributionRecord` connected to
the authorized actor, governed task, locked guide and policy versions, exact
submission/review lineage, and verified outcome.

## Consequences Consume Facts

Payment, points, reputation, reporting, datasets, model training, eligibility,
and other project-specific consequences consume trusted contribution facts.
They do not define or rewrite Workstream lifecycle truth.

## History Remains History

Historical plans and review records retain their original language. Current
entry and architecture documents own the corrected definition.
